Levy Line is a classification aid built on the general framework of Ghana's Income Tax Act, 2015 (Act 896, as amended), the Revenue Administration Act, 2016 (Act 915), the Value Added Tax Act, 2025 (Act 1151, effective 1 January 2026), and the Growth and Sustainability Levy Act as amended. The Sector Audit Reference tab is drawn from a working-draft sector audit workbook (prepared August 2026) — cross-check figures against the primary legislation and current GRA practice before relying on it in an engagement. Keyword-based classification and the pre-audit diagnostic are starting points, not filed positions. Your entries are stored only in your own workspace.
